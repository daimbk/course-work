What is Noisli?


Noisli is your digital place for focus.

With Noisli you can mix and match different sounds in
order to create your perfect sound environment.
We currently offer 28 high quality background sounds
that you can play individually or combine with each
other. You can adjust the volume for each sound in
order to customize your combinations to your needs
and make them your own.

Besides crafting your own Combos, you can also listen
to the carefully curated Playlists so to be inspired and
explore new sound suggestions for various situations.
We offer a Timer that helps you work in sessions in
order to prevent burnout, as well as a lovely
distraction-free Text Editor.

Discover all our Features here.

Noisli for Work


Staying focused at work is more and more a challenge.
Frequent interruptions, mainly in form of noise, are
breaking our focus, increasing our stress level and
lowering our productivity.

Use Noisli to drown out the overall office noise,
distracting phone conversations and the general
chatter from other people. Use our Timer to help you
work in sessions to maximise your efficiency and
prevent burnout.

Noisli works also great to create an inspiring
atmosphere that can boost your motivation and help
you think more creatively. Background sounds help
your mind from wandering around and keep you
concentrated on your task at hand.

Discover more about Noisli for Work here.

Noisli at Home


Working from home requires a lot of self-discipline as
you find yourself in an environment that you associate
with your private time and which is full of distractions
inviting you to procrastinate.

Most of the time it is also too quiet. Every spike in noise,
being it maybe your neighbours or your family
members, can bring you out of focus.

A lot of us turned on the TV or the radio to keep us
company and to indirectly mask other noises, but these
tools are not designed to do so and rather seek for our
attention. Noisli is the better option to drown out
sudden noises and to help you create an inspiring work
environment at home. You can either stream Noisli
through your speakers or use it with headphones to
drown out annoying noises.

Noisli while Studying


Noisli is a great tool to make studying more efficient.
Nobody can concentrate for a prolonged amount of
time, thus you can use Noisli to help you study in
smaller sessions using our Timer.

Our background sounds can help you to put yourself
into a relaxed study mode and to help you stay
concentrated for longer periods of time. This will allow
you to reduce the impact of outside distractions such
as phone or social media notifications and to study in a
more efficient way.

Noisli is also powerful in class, as it allows students to
find a calm space among all the other classmates. You
can use Noisli to enhance individual concentration or to
fuel creative writing by creating an inspiring
atmosphere.

Read more about Noisli for students and teachers here.

Noisli while Travelling


If you're a frequent traveller you will know that finding
an environment for peace and calm that lets you focus
is very challenging. Being it on your daily commute, on
the bus or train, or up in the air, peace and calm is a
rarity in such busy environments. You find yourself in
ever changing, buzzing places, where the noise
environment around you is out of your control.

Noisli can be your little helper to enter a familiar
workspace in order to get things done and stay
productive also on the go.
