4 digit display data pins connected to following Arduino ports:
pin 6 to pin 5
pin 8 to pin 6
pin 9 to pin 7
pin 12 to pin 8

Q0 - A
Q1 - B
Q2 - C
Q3 - D
Q4 - E
Q5 - F
Q6 - G
DS - pin 2
SH_CP - pin 3
ST_CP - pin 4

keypad row pins: 9 10 11 12
keypad column pins: A0 A1 A2 A3


4 digit anode 7-segment display mapping:
A = 11; B = 7; C = 4; D = 2; E = 1; F = 10; G = 5; DP = 3
